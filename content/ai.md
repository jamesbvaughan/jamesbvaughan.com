---
title: AI Disclaimer
description: >
  An overview of the extent of AI use involved in making this site.
---

There is no AI-generated writing or media on this website.

In my opinion, LLMs are excellent for proofreading, but posting AI-generated
writing with any implication that it was written by a human is
disrespectful to readers and makes the internet worse.

My use of AI on this site's content and source code is limited to:

- Light proofreading with prompts like "Review this post for typos, grammar
  mistakes, and factual errors."
- Debugging issues related to builds and deployments.
